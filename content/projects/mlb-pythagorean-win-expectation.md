Analyzed 100+ years of data from the Lahman Baseball Database to test Bill James' Pythagorean win expectation.

## Overview

- Engineered run-based features and compared Pythagorean exponents using MAE and R²
- Achieved R² > 0.85 for nearly all franchises; ranked all MLB franchises by model fit
- Authored a full statistical report in LaTeX covering methodology, evaluation, and limitations
